If you let me own something

Three leaks I would go and check, and how I would be wrong in front of you.

I have never worked inside CARS24, so these are hypotheses from public information. The method is the one I would use on day one: follow the money, mark where it leaks, then check it from outside before claiming anything.

  1. 1

    Where does a first-time seller outside the metros hesitate?

    Five to eight 10-minute calls with people who sold or nearly sold a car. Count the pattern. 'Five of seven said...'

    A 140-km field expedition across 12 villages, and a solo trip across all 28 states, are the closest things I have done.

  2. 2

    Where does information get lost at a handover?

    Walk one car end to end, from listing to keys, and mark every wait, repeat question and re-check.

    I did the same for assets at Akshaya Patra.

  3. 3

    Which AI-assisted step needs a hard rule?

    Pick one, write down what it must never claim, and test it against that.

    ResumeFit is built around exactly that rule.
